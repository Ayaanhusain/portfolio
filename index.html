01 — About

Certainty is hard. That's the fun part.

I'm an undergraduate at IIT Madras, working through the BS in Mathematics. Most of my time goes into problem sets, lecture notes, and side projects that begin with “what if…” — a proof, a simulation, a small program written to test whether an intuition survives contact with reality.

I'm most alive where these four things overlap: a single idea can be a theorem, a physical model, an algorithm, and a philosophical position all at once. Chasing that overlap is what I do.

I'm currently part of the Quant Guild and Cysec Guild at IIT Madras — spaces where the mathematical and computational edges of finance and security get pushed a little further.

Away from the desk I'm on the mat and under the bar — wrestling, training calisthenics, and lifting with NSO Weightlifting at IIT Madras. And lately, I've been learning chess — the quietest of all the disciplines so far.

05 — Currently learning

The board, one move at a time

I'm learning chess the slow way — tactics first, openings later. What keeps pulling me back is that it's the same act as mathematics: sit with a position, consider every reply, and only then move. The knight is the piece I understand least, so it's the one I practise most.
